How it works

Forty-six distinct moments for every birthday.

Equivalent fractions land on the same milestone, so 1/2, 2/4, and 6/12 count once. Twelfth-aligned markers keep each person’s birth day-of-month, capped at month end. Every other fraction belongs to the calendar day containing its exact position between anniversaries.
